A public record of the targets in sustainability-linked bonds, and of what issuers later reported.

A sustainability-linked bond pays investors more, usually through a higher coupon, if the issuer does not reach a sustainability target written into the bond's terms. The Open Promise Ledger records each of these targets, called promises here, and what the issuer or its named external reviewer later published about them: the reported figure, any statement on whether the target was met, and any statement that the payment change took effect.

Every line gives the document, the page and the exact quote. The Ledger repeats what issuers stated. It does not rate, score or rank issuers or instruments, does not assess their sustainability, and does not propose or suggest any investment decision.

How a line is made

The editor reads the bond's terms and the issuer's reports and records each value with its quote, and commits it before any AI system sees those documents. An AI system then reads the same documents as an error finder; a script checks that every quote it gives is on the cited page, and the editor settles every difference at the page. A blind second coder and an auditor check the result before anything is published. The full rules are in the Method.
