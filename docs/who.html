Responsibility

Until the project has a host institution or a legal entity, the editor alone is responsible for everything the Ledger publishes, and personally liable for it. The use of an AI system moves no responsibility away from him.

Use of AI

An AI system (Claude, by Anthropic) makes a second reading of the same documents to find errors, and fills some descriptive fields and draft translations after the editor's own reading; the editor checks each one on the page. Translations from languages other than English are marked as AI translated, and the original prevails. The AI decides no record and is not an author. Every release names the model, the settings and the number of runs.

Interests

The editor is a founder of Greenhold, a separate company. Greenhold has no rights in the Ledger, its Method or the Open Promise names, sees no record before publication and pays nothing toward it. From the publication of the sample until 30 days after each release, the editor neither holds nor trades securities of issuers in the frame. He holds no listed securities today.
