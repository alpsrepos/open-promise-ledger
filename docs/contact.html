Corrections and contact

If a line is wrong, please say which line and give the public source that shows the correct value. A line under review is marked as such within one working day and left out of all counts until it is resolved; the correction or a reasoned reply follows within five working days.

For now, please open an issue on GitHub. A project email address will be added here.

Please do not send information that is not already public; it cannot be used.
