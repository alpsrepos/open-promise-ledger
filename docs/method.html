Method

The Method sets out what is recorded, how the sample is drawn, the nine status labels and the order in which they are tested, how the AI is used and checked, and what each release reports. It is a draft for comment, version 0.2.

Read the Method (version 0.2) · Data dictionary

In short

WhatSustainability-linked bonds on the Euronext ESG bonds list with a target observed in 2024 or 2025
SampleIssuer groups (GLEIF ultimate parent) in a random order with a public seed, screened one by one; a declared pilot of three; ten groups in wave 1
RecordsFour linked tables: bond, promise, link (bond and promise, with the payment change and the status) and observation
Status labelsEnded before observation date; Cannot be compared (promise); Not yet due; Stated as reached or not reached; Reported figure reaches or does not reach the promised value; Cannot be compared (figure); Awaiting report; No figure recorded yet; No report found by the editor. A statement by the issuer or its external reviewer comes before any comparison of figures. Labels are never weighted, scored, coloured or combined.
ChecksA blind second coder (a different AI model that never sees the records); a test of the AI reading on cases it was not tuned on; a full check of every line by an AI auditor; with no outside person, the release is labelled "AI reading not validated"; 15 working days for issuers to correct a line with a public source
RegistrationThe frame and seed, then the protocol, deposited with a DOI before the data they govern
