Until the project has a host institution or a legal entity, the editor alone is responsible for everything the Ledger publishes, and personally liable for it. The use of an AI system moves no responsibility away from him.

Use of AI

An AI system (Claude, by Anthropic) reads each bond's documents first, twice, and proposes every value with its page and quote. The editor checks every value against the page, through the AI's translation where a document is not in a language he reads (those lines are marked), corrects it where needed, and decides each status. Translations from languages other than English are marked as AI translated, and the original prevails. Before the first full release, a blind second coder who uses no AI codes a sample of issuers, and an auditor checks every line. The AI also drafted the site texts, the notice texts and the code, which the editor reviewed. The AI is not an author. Every release names the model, the settings and the number of runs.
